What is WE1A?

WE1A, Inc. is a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering women impacted by incarceration. Since 2014, WE1A has provided structured programs within correctional institutions that help women build confidence, develop accountability, and prepare for successful transitions back into their communities. Founded by a leader with more than a decade of experience working directly within correctional environments, WE1A’s programs are built on practical experience and a trauma-informed approach that supports emotional growth, personal responsibility, and reentry readiness. As WE1A continues to grow, the organization is expanding its institutional partnerships and developing sustainable programs that support women beyond incarceration. Through collaboration with correctional facilities, WE1A provides empowerment cohorts focused on behavioral growth, workforce readiness, and successful reintegration. WE1A’s future initiatives include Divinity House Support Services, a planned transitional housing program designed to address housing instability and provide women with the structure and support needed to pursue employment, independence, and long-term stability. Through responsible growth, documented outcomes, and community partnerships, WE1A is building a continuum of support that strengthens reentry opportunities, reduces barriers to success, and helps create safer, more stable communities.
